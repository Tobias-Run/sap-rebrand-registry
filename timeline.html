How to read this

Each row is one product's full chain of names, drawn to scale against the same time axis. A segment's color names the transition that started it — a plain rename, an assimilation after an acquisition, or a technology generation change — the same three kinds as on the register. The first period of a chain has no transition and carries no color; it is where the row's story starts, not a change.

The SAP ERP row at the top is pinned there on purpose. Its three eras — SAP R/3, mySAP ERP, SAP ERP — are the longest-running, most recognizable frame SAP's own history offers, so the chart uses them as a background ruler: every other row's bars sit against faint bands marking which ERP era was current underneath them.

Generation changes are muted by default, matching the register's rule that they sit outside the statistics — turn on "Highlight generation changes" to see them in their own color instead. Every value shown here on hover or focus is also on the register as plain text, with its source.
